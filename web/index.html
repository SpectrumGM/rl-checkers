AlphaZero Checkers

Play against a checkers engine that taught itself with a neural network and Monte Carlo Tree Search, or against classic minimax opponents for comparison. Source on GitHub

New game

Choose an opponent and press Start.
    How to play. Click one of your pieces, then a green dot. Captures are mandatory and multi-jumps are made in one click. A game is drawn after 40 moves without a capture.

    The bar on the left is the network's own estimate of who is winning (white = good for White).
